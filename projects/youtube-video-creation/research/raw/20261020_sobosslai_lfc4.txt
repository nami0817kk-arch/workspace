Dominik Szoboszlai was ruled out of Liverpool's Premier League meeting with Manchester City because of a hamstring injury, Andoni Iraola has confirmed.
The issue meant the No.8 was not part of the Reds' matchday squad at Anfield.
Iraola told Sky Sports pre-match: "Another one coming from the international games. He came with an overload, he felt a tight hamstring.
"He's tried somehow, kind of pushing through training, but he was not feeling 100 per cent and we will have to wait for Dom, yes."
