Harry Kane Is Closing In on Cristiano Ronaldo in the Champions League

  • The Bayern Munich striker has now scored in 10 consecutive Champions League games and stands just one match away from Cristiano Ronaldo’s all-time record.

Kane Is Now Within Touching Distance of Cristiano Ronaldo

Harry Kane scored again for Bayern Munich against Arsenal, extending his scoring streak to 10 consecutive matches in the UEFA Champions League. After the game, the England striker reflected on a run that has put him on the verge of history, just one match short of Cristiano Ronaldo’s record of scoring in 11 straight Champions League games.
