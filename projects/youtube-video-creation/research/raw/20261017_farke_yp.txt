Robbie Evans on why Leeds United are taking time over Daniel Farke's new contract
By 
Stuart Rayner
Chief Football Writer
Published 
11th Sep 2026, 07:00 BST
Managing director Robbie Evans says Leeds United are taking time to get Daniel Farke's next contract right, but supporters should not be alarmed they have not got there yet.
Farke is in the final year of his 
Elland Road
 contract, with his stock as high as it has been in that time after returning them to the 
Premier League
 in 2025, consolidating them there in the first season back, and now building on a positive transfer window with an unbeaten start to the 2026-27 league campaign.
Whilst loathe to go into details, Farke this week hinted there could be progress in September/October's international window but Evans says there is no rush on his part, and people should not read anything negative into that.

"He's done great, we want him to be here long-term, and we're working on it," he said of a manager starting his fourth season at the club.
CONTRACT TALKS: Leeds United manager Daniel Farke (Image: Andrew Redington/Getty Images)
"Daniel's done a fantastic job here and we're thrilled with the last few seasons, want to keep it going. He's part of the long-term project.
"We're tying to find a solution within the long-term.
"Daniel's done a fantastic job here, and we're thrilled with the last couple of seasons. We want to keep it going, keep building the long-term project. And so we were trying to find a solution with him long-term.
“The ongoing operating relationship between Daniel and Adam (Underwood, the sporting director) and myself has never been better. The level of collaboration and coordination this summer was truly excellent. Getting our top (transfer) targets in, talking every day.
INTERNATIONAL RETIREMENT: Noah Okafor (Image: Alex Grimm/Getty Images)
"We're delivering at a really high level.
"Daniel and I, Daniel's team and I, have had really good chats for the last three months and we're all on the same page as far as what we want to accomplish, the long-term project. We simply haven't got the deal done yet, but we're working towards a solution.
“Extensions might feel like they are relatively straightforward, because you're both at the same company already, but I think of them as being quite difficult, even more than first-time signings, because unlike signing him back in (20)23 or doing a deadline-day deal, the time pressure is far reduced. We have the time to get it right as far as what he wants and what we want, and to deliver on that long-term project.
"Ethan (Ampadu)'s deal took over a year. We tried to get it down last spring, last summer, then last autumn, then in January and it wasn't done until June. (Joe) Rodon, (Jayden) Bogle, (Brenden) Aaronson, all those guys took months to finish.
"I wouldn’t read into it taking us some time as a problem. It just means we're trying to get it right, because we have time to get it right.
"As long as we're still operating together as a group I'm not worried, and we're doing great on that front.
"We have a great operating model, and we want to keep that going. We all know how to best work together."
Leeds have no immediate fresh injury concerns on the back of Wednesday's dramatic 6-3 League Cup defeat to Chelsea for Monday's league match at home to Newcastle United.
Meanwhile, all bar one of Leeds and Hull City's Festive Premier League fixtures have been moved for the benefit of television after the league responded to criticism of the short notice of last year's game changes by confirming these before November's TV matches have been selected.
Both clubs will play on Boxing Day, with Leeds kicking off at Aston Villa at 12.30pm, and Hull's home game with Liverpool at 5.30pm.
The Yorkshire derby at the MKM Stadium has been brought forward to 7.30pm on December 29.
Leeds' New Year's Day game at home to Everton and the January 6 visit of Manchester City will kick-off at 8pm and 8.15pm respectively.
Nottingham Forest v Hull will now kick off at 7.30pm on January 5. All bar the Villa-Leeds game, which is on TNT Sports, will be shown live on Sky Sports.
Noah Okafor has retired from international football after playing just 42 minutes (plus stoppage time) of Switzerland's World Cup campaign.
