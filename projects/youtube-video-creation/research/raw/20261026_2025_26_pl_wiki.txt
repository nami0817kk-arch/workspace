The '''2025–26 Premier League''' was the 34th season of the [[Premier League]] and the 127th season of {{nowrap|top-flight}} [[Football in England|English football]]. The fixtures were released on 18 June 2025.<ref>{{#invoke:cite|web|title=Dates for 2025/26 Premier League season confirmed playing a total of 380 matches |url=https://www.premierleague.com/news/4171848 |date=22 November 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20241126195919/https://www.premierleague.com/news/4171848 |archive-date=26 November 2024 |access-date=18 March 2025 |website=PremierLeague.com |publisher=Premier League}}</ref> The season consisted of 33 weekend and five midweek rounds of matches.

[[Liverpool F.C.|Liverpool]] were the [[2024–25 Premier League|defending champions]], but were unable to defend their title, with [[Arsenal F.C.|Arsenal]] winning their fourth Premier League title and 14th English title with one match to spare, ending their 22-year Premier League drought since they last won in [[2003–04 FA Premier League|2004]], following [[Manchester City F.C.|Manchester City]]'s 1–1 draw away against [[AFC Bournemouth|Bournemouth]] on 19 May 2026, combined with their 1–0 win at home against [[Burnley]] the previous day.<ref name="SKY1">{{Cite web |title=Arsenal win Premier League: Mikel Arteta's side end 22-year wait for title |url=https://www.skysports.com/football/news/11670/13545591/arsenal-win-premier-league-mikel-artetas-side-end-22-year-wait-for-title |access-date=19 May 2026 |website=Sky Sports |language=en |archive-date=21 May 2026 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260521045744/https://www.skysports.com/football/news/11670/13545591/arsenal-win-premier-league-mikel-artetas-side-end-22-year-wait-for-title |url-status=live }}</ref>

This was the first season to feature the [[Tyne–Wear derby]] in the Premier League since the [[2015–16 Premier League|2015–16 season]], following [[Sunderland A.F.C.|Sunderland]]'s promotion via the [[English Football League play-offs|Championship play-offs]].

The summer transfer window opened on 16 June 2025 and closed at 19:00 [[British Summer Time|BST]] on 1 September 2025.<ref>{{#invoke:cite|web|date=27 March 2025 |title=Summer 2025 Transfer Window dates and times confirmed |url=https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4272739 |access-date=9 September 2025 |website=PremierLeague.com |publisher=Premier League}}</ref> The winter window opened on 1 January 2026 and closed at 19:00 [[Greenwich Mean Time|GMT]] on 2 February 2026.

== Summary ==
=== Title race ===
The early pace-setters were [[Liverpool F.C.|Liverpool]], who won their first five games and spent nearly all of August and September combined on top of the Premier League table.<ref name="ATH1">{{cite news |last1=O'Neil |first1=Conor |title=The 2025–26 Premier League title race explained |url=https://www.nytimes.com/athletic/7201419/2026/04/19/premier-league-title-race-manchester-city-arsenal/ |access-date=21 May 2026 |publisher=The Athletic |date=19 April 2026}}</ref> However, Arsenal went top of the table on 4 October after a 2–0 win over 
[[West Ham United F.C.|West Ham United]] was followed on the same day by a 2–1 defeat for Liverpool against [[Chelsea F.C.|Chelsea]]; they would remain virtually top for the rest of the season,<ref name="NBC1">{{cite news |last1=Mendola |first1=Nicholas |title=How Arsenal won the Premier League |url=https://www.nbcsports.com/soccer/news/how-arsenal-won-the-premier-league-the-path-from-a-week-1-escape-to-a-week-37-crown |access-date=21 May 2026 |publisher=NBC Sports |date=20 May 2026 |archive-date=21 May 2026 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260521025140/https://www.nbcsports.com/soccer/news/how-arsenal-won-the-premier-league-the-path-from-a-week-1-escape-to-a-week-37-crown |url-status=live }}</ref> leading the table for a total of 238 days.<ref name="GAR1">{{cite news |last1=Hopkins |first1=Oliver |title=The 10 numbers that sum up how Arsenal won the Premier League |url=https://www.theguardian.com/football/2026/may/21/numbers-sum-up-arsenal-won-premier-league |access-date=21 May 2026 |work=The Guardian |date=21 May 2026}}</ref> Their lead from [[Manchester City F.C.|Manchester City]] fluctuated and at one point on 22 April, City were ahead of Arsenal on goals scored.<ref name="NBC1" /> However, Arsenal won their next four games and with City's draws at [[Everton F.C.|Everton]] on 4 May and [[AFC Bournemouth|Bournemouth]] on 19 May, they were confirmed champions with a game to spare.<ref name="SKY1" />

On 24 May, Arsenal were crowned champions after being presented with the Premier League trophy after a 2–1 away win against [[Crystal Palace F.C.|Crystal Palace]] on the final day of the season.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/football/live/c3d20dk33emt|title=Crystal Palace 1-2 Arsenal|date=24 May 2026|work=BBC Sport|accessdate=25 May 2026}}</ref>

=== Relegation ===
[[Wolverhampton Wanderers F.C.|Wolverhampton Wanderers]] became the first team to be relegated to the [[EFL Championship]] on 20 April 2026, after a goalless draw between West Ham United and Crystal Palace combined with their 3–0 loss away to Leeds United two days previously.<ref name=wolves-rel>{{cite web|url=https://www.espn.com/soccer/story/_/id/48491722/wolves-relegated-premier-league-west-ham-crystal-palace-draw|title=Wolves relegated from Premier League as West Ham, Crystal Palace draw|work=ESPN|date=20 April 2026|accessdate=20 May 2026|archive-date=20 May 2026|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260520195332/https://www.espn.com/soccer/story/_/id/48491722/wolves-relegated-premier-league-west-ham-crystal-palace-draw|url-status=live}}</ref> This ended an eight-year stay in the Premier League,<ref name=wolves-rel /> and also meant that this would be the first time since the [[2022–23 Premier League|2022–23 season]] where at least one promoted club avoided immediate relegation, after all three of the promoted teams went straight back down in [[2023–24 Premier League|2023–24]] and [[2024–25 Premier League|2024–25]].<ref name=sund&leeds /><ref name="opta-relegation"/>

[[Burnley F.C|Burnley]] became the second team to be relegated to the [[EFL Championship]] on 22 April 2026 following their 1–0 loss at home to [[Manchester City F.C.|Manchester City]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.espn.com/soccer/story/_/id/48562938/burnley-relegated-premier-league-2025-2026|title=Burnley relegated from Premier League after single season|work=ESPN|date=22 April 2026|accessdate=20 May 2026|archive-date=20 May 2026|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260520195332/https://www.espn.com/soccer/story/_/id/48562938/burnley-relegated-premier-league-2025-2026|url-status=live}}</ref>

The last relegation spot was decided on the final day of the season between London [[Tottenham Hotspur F.C.–West Ham United F.C. rivalry|rivals]] [[West Ham United F.C.|West Ham United]] and [[Tottenham Hotspur F.C.|Tottenham Hotspur]], with Spurs having a two point advantage. West Ham defeated [[Leeds United F.C.|Leeds United]] 3–0 in their last match, but due to Tottenham also beating [[Everton F.C.|Everton]], West Ham became the third team to be relegated.<ref>{{Cite web |date=24 May 2026 |title=West Ham relegated despite win over Leeds on Final Day |url=https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4662359/west-ham-relegated-despite-win-over-leeds-on-final-day |access-date=24 May 2026 |website=www.premierleague.com |language=en |archive-date=26 May 2026 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260526150529/https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4662359/west-ham-relegated-despite-win-over-leeds-on-final-day |url-status=live }}</ref> This was the first season since [[2010–11 Premier League|2010–11]] that a club was relegated with as many as 39 points.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Tottenham avoid relegation from Premier League with tense win over Everton |url=https://www.espn.co.uk/football/story/_/id/48804476/tottenham-salvage-premier-league-status-avoid-relegation |access-date=2026-05-30 |website=www.espn.co.uk |archive-date=27 May 2026 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260527093823/https://www.espn.co.uk/football/story/_/id/48804476/tottenham-salvage-premier-league-status-avoid-relegation |url-status=live }}</ref>

=== Promoted clubs ===

==Teams==
Twenty teams competed in the league – the top seventeen teams from the previous season and three promoted from the [[2024–25 EFL Championship|Championship]]. The promoted teams were [[Leeds United F.C.|Leeds United]], [[Burnley F.C.|Burnley]], and [[Sunderland A.F.C.|Sunderland]], returning to the top flight after absences of two, one, and eight years, respectively. They replaced [[Leicester City F.C.|Leicester City]], [[Ipswich Town F.C.|Ipswich Town]], and [[Southampton F.C.|Southampton]], who were all relegated to the [[2025–26 EFL Championship|Championship]] after one year in the top flight. This marked the second consecutive season, and only the third time in Premier League history, in which all three promoted teams were relegated after just one season.<ref name="opta-relegation">{{#invoke:cite|web|last=Tweedale |first=Ali |date=26 April 2025 |title=The Three Promoted Premier League Sides Will Be Relegated Again – This Never Used to Happen |url=https://theanalyst.com/2025/04/premier-league-promoted-sides-relegation-championship |access-date=27 April 2025 |website=Opta Analyst }}</ref>
