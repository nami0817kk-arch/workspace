Morgan Elliot Rogers
 (born 26 July 2002) is an English professional 
footballer
 who plays as an 
attacking midfielder
 or 
winger
 for 
Premier League
 club 
Chelsea
 and the 
England national team
. A versatile player across attacking positions, Rogers is known for his strength, attacking runs, ability to score from a distance, and ball control.
[
6
]
[
7
]
[
8
]
[
9
]


Born in 
Halesowen
 in the 
Dudley Metropolitan Borough
, Rogers is a product of the 
West Bromwich Albion
 academy. He signed for 
Manchester City
 in 2019, but did not make an appearance for the club. After loan spells at 
Lincoln City
, 
Bournemouth
, and 
Blackpool
, he joined 
Middlesbrough
 in 2023. After half a season at the club, Rogers signed for 
Aston Villa
 for a reported fee of £8 million in 2024. Following a breakout season with Villa in which he was named their young player of the season, Rogers won the 
PFA Young Player of the Year
 award in 2025. In July 2026, he joined Chelsea for a fee of £117 million, a record for an English player.


Rogers has represented England at international levels from under-15 to senior, making his senior international debut in 2024.
[
10
]
[
11
]
 In May 2026, he made the squad for the 
2026 FIFA World Cup
.
[
12
]


Early life
[
edit
]


Born in 
Halesowen
 to Deborah and Howard Rogers,
[
13
]
[
14
]
 Rogers attended Colley Lane Primary and 
Sandwell Academy
,
[
13
]
 and was part of the Sandwell team that won the National Schools Cup in 2017, scoring and assisting in the final.
[
15
]
 Rogers was a childhood fan of 
West Bromwich Albion
[
16
]
 and a childhood friend of fellow footballer 
Jude Bellingham
.
[
17
]


Club career
[
edit
]


West Bromwich Albion
[
edit
]


Rogers joined West Bromwich Albion at the age of seven, after being spotted by a club scout.
[
15
]
 He made his professional debut for West Brom on 6 February 2019, coming on as an 82nd-minute substitute for 
Wes Hoolahan
 against 
Brighton & Hove Albion
 in the 
FA Cup
.
[
18
]


Manchester City
[
edit
]


Rogers signed for 
Manchester City
 on 1 August 2019.
[
19
]


Lincoln City (loan)
[
edit
]


On 4 January 2021, Rogers signed for 
Lincoln City
 on loan until the end of the season.
[
20
]
 He made his debut for the club against 
Peterborough United
 a few days later, coming off the bench.
[
21
]
 He scored his first professional goal against 
Portsmouth
 in a 1–0 win on 26 January.
[
22
]
 Three goals in seven games during the month of March contributed to his winning the 
EFL Young Player of the Month
 award during his loan spell.
[
23
]


Bournemouth (loan)
[
edit
]


On 23 August 2021, Rogers signed for 
Bournemouth
 on loan until the end of the season.
[
24
]
 Rogers found his first-team opportunities at Bournemouth limited, and was confined to mostly substitute appearances during the season.
[
25
]
 He scored his only goal for the club on 15 January 2022, in a 3–2 
Championship
 defeat against 
Luton Town
.
[
26
]


Blackpool (loan)
[
edit
]


On 4 January 2023, Rogers joined 
Blackpool
 on loan for the remainder of the season.
[
27
]
 He scored his only goal for the club in a 1–0 win against 
Norwich City
 on 8 May 2023.
[
28
]


Middlesbrough
[
edit
]


On 7 July 2023, Rogers signed for Championship club 
Middlesbrough
 for an undisclosed fee.
[
29
]
 He scored his first goal for the club on 29 July, a late equaliser to make it 2–2 in a 
pre-season friendly
 against 
Auxerre
.
[
30
]
 He scored his first competitive goal for the club on 29 August in a 3–1 
EFL Cup
 win against 
Bolton Wanderers
.
[
31
]


Rogers scored five times during Middlesbrough's run to the 
EFL Cup
 semi-finals, making him the competition's top goalscorer for the season.
[
32
]


Aston Villa
[
edit
]


Rogers playing for Aston Villa in 2026


On 1 February 2024, 21-year-old Rogers signed for 
Premier League
 club 
Aston Villa
 for an undisclosed fee,
[
33
]
 reported to be £8m, potentially rising to £15m with performance-related add-ons.
[
34
]
 Manchester City were entitled to 25% of the fee, due to a sell-on clause in the original transfer to Middlesbrough.
[
35
]
 On 3 February, Rogers made his Premier League debut as a late substitute in a 5–0 victory away at 
Sheffield United
.
[
36
]


On 6 April 2024, Rogers scored his first goal for Aston Villa in a 3–3 draw against 
Brentford
.
[
37
]
 On 29 January 2025, he netted his first 
UEFA Champions League
 goals by scoring a 
hat-trick
, including two in the first five minutes, in a 4–2 victory over 
Celtic
 in which he was named player of the match.
[
38
]
[
39
]
[
40
]
 In doing so, he became the second youngest English player to score a hat-trick in the competition.
[
41
]
 On April 10, 2025, Aston Villa lost 3–1 to 
Paris Saint-Germain
 in the 
Champions League quarterfinal
 first leg; Rogers scored Aston Villa's only goal.
[
42
]


After a breakout 
2024–25 season
 in which he scored 14 goals and provided 15 assists across all competitions,
[
43
]
 Rogers was named Aston Villa's Young Player of the Season at the club's end of season awards.
[
44
]
 On 19 June 2025, it was announced that Rogers was one of six nominees for the 
PFA Young Player of the Year
 award,
[
45
]
 which he then went on to win – becoming the fifth Aston Villa player to win the award since its inception.
[
46
]


On 10 November 2025, Rogers signed a contract extension with Aston Villa until 2031.
[
47
]
 On 20 May 2026, he scored in a 3–0 win over 
SC Freiburg
 in the 
Europa League final
 as his club won the competition for the first time in their history.
[
48
]
 
UEFA
 subsequently named Rogers the 
Europa League Player of the Season
.
[
49
]


Chelsea
[
edit
]


On 21 July 2026, Rogers joined Premier League club 
Chelsea
.
[
50
]
 The fee of £117 million for Rogers became the record for an English footballer.
[
51
]


On 24 August 2026, Rogers scored his first Premier League goal for Chelsea with a 3–2 victory over 
Fulham
 in the opening round.
[
52
]
 In the third round of the 
EFL Cup
 against 
Leeds United
 on 9 September, Rogers was introduced as a half-time substitute with Chelsea trailing 0–1, and set up four goals as the club fought back to win 6–3.
[
53
]

 at

 On 22 March 2024, Rogers made his debut for the

 in

 during

Senior
[
edit
]


Rogers with 
England
 at the 
2026 FIFA World Cup


On 11 November 2024, Rogers was called up to the 
England senior team
 for the first time for upcoming 
UEFA Nations League
 fixtures against 
Greece
 and the 
Republic of Ireland
.
[
61
]
 The 22-year-old made his senior debut on 14 November as a second-half substitute in a 3–0 away victory against Greece.
[
62
]
[
63
]
 Rogers scored his first England goal in a 3–0 
friendly
 win over 
Wales
 at 
Wembley
 on 9 October 2025.
[
64
]


On 22 May 2026, Rogers was selected in the 26-man squad for the 
2026 FIFA World Cup
.

12

Style of play
[
edit
]


Rogers is a versatile player who primarily operated as a 
number 10
 at Aston Villa
[
65
]
 but can also play as a winger, striker or attacking midfielder.
[
8
]
 Tactically, he is known to take long distance shots
[
66
]
 and engage in an aggressive style of attacking play, driving at opponents and breaking defensive lines.
[

]

 He has received praise for his physical strength,

 tactical intelligence,

 ability to score from a distance,
[

[

 dribbling
[

[

 and work ethic.
[

 His strength on the ball and dangerous runs have drawn media comparisons to 
Jamal Musiala
, 
Ronaldo
 and 
Wayne Rooney
.
[

]
[

[

 Rogers himself has expressed admiration for Musiala, as well as

 and Villa teammate

Described throughout 2025 as one of the best young players in the Premier League,
[

]
[

 media outlets began describing Rogers as one of the best attacking midfielders in the world during the

 ranking him eighth in his position.
[

[
81

Sports Illustrated
 described Rogers as an "anomaly" among attacking midfielders due to his physical strength,
[
81

 while 
ESPN
 included Rogers in twelfth on their 2025 
FC 100
 list of the best attacking midfielders in the world, writing that Rogers "has it all – youth, power, intensity, skills, hunger, energy, confidence".
[

 In the same year 
GiveMeSport
 wrote that his ball control was "one of, if not the best in the league".
[
9

 Owing to Rogers' performances, some media sources opined that Rogers could start for England at the

 out of the starting eleven;
