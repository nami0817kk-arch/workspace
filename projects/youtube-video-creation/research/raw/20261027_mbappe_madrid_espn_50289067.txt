Harry Kane became the first Englishman in 25 years to win the Ballon d'Or as he took home the main prize at the 2026 ceremony in London.
Taking place in England's capital rather than the traditional Paris in honour of Stanley Matthews' triumph 70-years-ago, Kane took the main men's prize home to follow on from Sir Stanely Matthews, Sir Bobby Charlton, Kevin Keegan and Michael Owen.
He was joined by Alexia Putellas, who won her third women's Ballon d'Or.
