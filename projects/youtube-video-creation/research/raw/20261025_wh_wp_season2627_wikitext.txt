The '''2026–27 season''' is the 132nd season in the history of [[West Ham United F.C.|West Ham United Football Club]], and the club's first season in the [[EFL Championship|Championship]] since the [[2011–12 West Ham United F.C. season|2011–12 season]] following their relegation from the [[Premier League]] in the [[2025–26 West Ham United F.C. season|preceding season]]. In addition to the [[2026–27 EFL Championship|domestic league]], the club are also participating in the [[2026–27 FA Cup|FA Cup]] and [[2026–27 EFL Cup|EFL Cup]].

== Season Summary ==

=== August ===
West Ham kicked off their season in the [[EFL Cup]] first round, where they faced fellow Championship side [[Portsmouth F.C.|Portsmouth]]. The Hammers went behind in the first half before a goal each by [[Taty Castellanos]] and [[Jarrod Bowen]] just before half-time saw West Ham go into the break with a lead. Another goal from Castellanos early in the second half made it 3–1 which was enough to see them progress to round 2.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Text |first=Live |title=West Ham United 3-1 Portsmouth: Castellanos and Bowen on target as Hammers win |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/live/cx2v474wl3yt |access-date=2026-08-20 |website=BBC Sport |language=en-GB}}</ref>

The first league match of the season was against [[Burnley F.C.|Burnley]], away at [[Turf Moor]]. Burnley had been relegated along with West Ham at the end of the previous season. The Hammers took a 2–0 lead after [[Maximilian Kilman|Max Kilman]] scored his first goal for the club when heading in Bowen's first half corner, before [[Manor Solomon]] got a debut goal in the second half. The Hammers held a two-goal advantage until the 83rd minute before two late goals by [[Zian Flemming]] (including a penalty) for the home side saw Burnley come back to level at 2–2, which is how the match finished.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Text |first=Live |title=Burnley 2-2 West Ham - Clarets fight back to draw in Championship |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/live/cq70g50pj0x5t |access-date=2026-08-20 |website=BBC Sport |language=en-GB}}</ref>

West Ham's first home league game was a London derby against near neighbours [[Charlton Athletic F.C.|Charlton Athletic]]. This was the first league meeting between the two clubs since February 2007, when both teams were in the [[Premier League]]<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |last=Text |first=Live |title=West Ham United 1-2 Charlton Athletic: Castellanos misses added-time penalty as Addicks win |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/live/cw77g0zdlzj4t |access-date=2026-08-25 |website=BBC Sport |language=en-GB}}</ref>. The visitors took the lead after only 7 minutes through captain [[Lloyd Jones (English footballer)|Lloyd Jones]], who scored with an overhead kick following a long throw-in<ref name=":0" />. [[Tyreece Campbell]] doubled Charlton's lead on 56 after a mistake by [[Konstantinos Mavropanos]] allowed Campbell to dispossess him, before dribbling and shooting into the corner from outside the box<ref name=":0" />. West Ham dominated possession and debutant [[Joël Piroe|Joel Piroe]] scored on 63 minutes, shooting high into the roof of the net after Castellanos' initial shot was blocked by the Charlton goalkeeper. West Ham had the chance to level the match when they were awarded a 93rd minute penalty, after former Hammer [[Conor Coventry]] handballed in the box; however, Castellanos hit the post with his kick and the match ended in a 1–2 defeat for West Ham.<ref name=":0" />

A much changed West Ham side travelled to [[St Mary's Stadium|St. Mary's]]  to face [[Southampton F.C.|Southampton]] in the 2nd round of the EFL Cup to. The Hammers again found themselves behind early on when [[Léo Scienza|Leo Scienza]] picked up a clearance and dribbled into the box before shooting low into the goal.<ref name=":1">{{Cite web |last=Text |first=Live |title=Southampton 1-4 West Ham: Hammers beat the Saints in Carabao Cup |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/live/cqe8k5mpm407t |access-date=2026-08-26 |website=BBC Sport |language=en-GB}}</ref> West Ham found an equaliser in he 41st minute when 19 year-old academy prospect [[Joshua Ajala|Josh Ajala]] headed in [[Lewis Orford (footballer)|Lewis Orford's]] corner at the back post for his first senior goal.<ref name=":1" /> Ajala added a second goal on 53, firing into the bottom corner after good work from [[Divine Mukasa]] on the left.<ref name=":1" /> West Ham brought on Taty Castellanos and Bowen, both of whom made an impact - Taty making it 3–1 to the Hammers with a tap in from [[Joël Veltman|Joel Veltman's]] cutback, before Bowen scored West Ham's fourth in the 84th minute after receiving Orford's pass then cutting inside on his left foot and shooting into the corner.<ref name=":1" /> The final score was 4–1 to West Ham, who progressed to round 3.<ref name=":1" />

West Ham made the short journey to [[Watford F.C.|Watford]] on 29th August. Once again, the Hammers found themselves behind early on when [[Amin Nabizada]] scored a solo effort after 20 minutes. West Ham were only behind for 7 minutes before Castellanos pressured [[Kévin Keben|Kevin Keben]] on Watford's right, forcing a mistake before [[Mattie Pollock]] turned the Argentine striker's low ball from the byline into his own net for 1–1. The Hammers dominated possession from then on and had chances to take the lead through Piroe, Solomon, new signing [[Arne Engels]] and Bowen but they could not find a winner and the match ended in a draw, meaning West Ham were still searching for their first league win of the season.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Text |first=Live |title=Watford 1-1 West Ham: Hammers still to win in Championship |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/live/cky8zxyj73j2t |access-date=2026-09-04 |website=BBC Sport |language=en-GB}}</ref>

August was concluded with a home match against another side relegated from the Premier League in [[Wolverhampton Wanderers]].<ref name=":2">{{Cite web |last=Text |first=Live |title=West Ham United 4-2 Wolverhampton Wanderers: Hammers off the mark |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/live/cm70d465j4yyt |access-date=2026-09-04 |website=BBC Sport |language=en-GB}}</ref> The visitors had the chance to go top of the table with a win and were unbeaten so far in the season.<ref name=":2" /> Wolves had the better of the early play as West Ham struggled, with [[Raúl Jiménez|Raul Jimenez]] going close on two occasions and [[Jackson Tchatchoua]] also missing a good chance<ref name=":2" />. [[Mateus Mané|Mateus Mane]] also hit the bar for Wolves before Mukasa scored a spectacular effort from 25-yards to put West Ham 1–0 up against the run of play on 33 minutes. This swung momentum in the Hammers' favour and Bowen made it 2–0 from the penalty spot on 40 after Piroe was fouled in the box.<ref name=":2" /> Wolves pulled a goal back two minutes before half time when [[Marshall Munetsi]] outpaced Mavropanos and took the ball on to score with a low shot.<ref name=":2" /> West Ham restored their 2-goal advantage in the 53rd minute when a fortunate deflection saw Mukasa's shot loop into the goal over [[José Sá|Jose Sa]] for his second of the game, after collecting Bowen's pass.<ref name=":2" /> Three became four when Mavropanos slammed the ball into the roof of the net from close range following a corner; however, the [[Greeks|Greek]] [[Defender (association football)|defender]] then turned in [[Jordan James (footballer, born 2004)|Jordan James]]' low cross for an own goal to reduce the deficit again. The final score was 4–2 to West Ham as they picked up their first league win of the campaign and inflicted Wolves' first defeat.<ref name=":2" />

[[Adama Boiro]] signed on transfer deadline day, on loan from [[Athletic Bilbao|Atletic Bilbao]]. He joined Engels, Veltman, Solomon, [[Keiber Lamadrid]] and [[Edvin Austbø|Edvin Austbo]] as major signings in the transfer window. The likes of [[El Hadji Malick Diouf|El-Hadji Malick Diouf]], [[Crysencio Summerville]] and [[Mateus Fernandes (footballer)|Mateus Fernandes]] were among those players to leave for large fees, with [[James Ward-Prowse]] also returning to former club Southampton on a permanent deal and [[Niclas Füllkrug|Niclas Fullkrug]], [[Aaron Wan-Bissaka]] and [[Jean-Clair Todibo]] all joining other clubs on long-term loans. 

=== September ===
The month began with a visit from [[Derby County F.C.|Derby County]] to the London Stadium. West Ham took the lead early through Piroe, who tapped in on the goal line after Taty had headed Bowen's cross from the right back across the goalmouth. West Ham dominated the possession and chances but took until the 87th minute to find a second goal when substitute Austbo scored from close range after Bowen's attempt from Engels pass had rolled into his path unmarked and he scored with a low shot, his first goal for the club. [[Mohamadou Kanté|Mohamadou Kante]] put the result beyond any doubt in stoppage time when he scored with a low drive from 20-yards, which was also his first West Ham goal as the Hammers won comfortably 3–0.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Text |first=Live |title=West Ham 3-0 Derby County: Hammers earn back-to-back wins |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/live/c6n9w3qy5ezjt |access-date=2026-09-09 |website=BBC Sport |language=en-GB}}</ref>

West Ham faced [[Bolton Wanderers]] in an away match on 8 September. This was the first league meeting between the two clubs since April 2011 when both were in the Premier League.<ref name=":3">{{Cite web |last=football |first=James ChettleGraduate Sports Journalist with a love for |date=2026-09-07 |title=Bolton vs West Ham H2H: Record, last meeting and previous results |url=https://readwestham.com/2026/09/07/bolton-west-ham-h2h/ |access-date=2026-09-09 |website=Read West Ham F.C. |language=en-GB}}</ref> An entertaining first half saw West Ham take the lead on 13 minutes when Bowen fired high into the net after Bolton looked to have cleared any danger. The match turned around following two Bolton goals in just under 10 minutes from [[Ethan Erhahon|Ethan Erhahon's]] smart low shot and a penalty from [[Rúben Rodrigues (footballer, born 1996)|Ruben Rodrigues]]. The Hammers restored parity four minutes later when Engels’deep free kick from the right found Mavropanos unmarked at the far post to head in. Bowen scored a winner with just under a quarter of hour of normal time remaining when he steered Veltman's through ball inside the near post in an effort which [[Gavin Bazunu|Bazunu]] in the Bolton goal failed to keep out.<ref name=":4">{{Cite web |last=Text |first=Live |title=Bowen double helps West Ham beat Bolton to go top |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/live/cv4g58j208lrt |access-date=2026-09-09 |website=BBC Sport |language=en-GB}}</ref> the 3–2 victory for West Ham was their first league win against Bolton since May 2007<ref name=":4" /> and their first ever win at Bolton's current stadium in any competition; the Hammers previous league victory at Bolton came in 1995 at [[Burnden Park]].<ref name=":3" />

West Ham faced [[Wrexham A.F.C.|Wrexham]] in their next league match at the London Stadium on 11 September . The Hammers recorded a 6–0 victory, their fourth consecutive league win, to move to the top of the Championship. Jarrod Bowen opened the scoring in the 28th minute, converting [[Morato (footballer, born 2001)|Morato’s]] low cross, before  Piroe doubled the lead after being set up by Castellanos seven minutes before half-time.<ref name=":5">{{Cite web |title=West Ham 6-0 Wrexham: Hammers go top of the Championship with fourth straight league win |url=https://www.skysports.com/football/west-ham-vs-wrexham/report/560581 |access-date=2026-09-24 |website=Sky Sports |language=en}}</ref> Mavropanos headed in Bowen’s corner three minutes after the restart, while Taty completed a counter-attack involving [[Kyle Walker-Peters]] to make it 4–0 in the 63rd minute. Kanté added a fifth after running onto Solomon’s pass, before Solomon completed the scoring in the 82nd minute. The result marked the first time in West Ham’s history in which six different players had scored for the club in a league match and it was also West Ham's biggest league win since April 2012.<ref name=":5" /><ref>{{Cite web |title=Match Report {{!}} Hammers hit Wrexham for six on record-breaking Friday night - West Ham United FC |url=https://www.whufc.com/en/news/match-report-or-hammers-hit-wrexham-for-six-on-record-breaking-friday-night |access-date=2026-09-24 |website=www.whufc.com |language=en}}</ref>

Four days later, West Ham hosted [[Fulham F.C.|Fulham]] in the third round of the EFL Cup. [[Rodrigo Muniz (Brazilian footballer)|Rodrigo Muniz]] headed Fulham into the lead in the 13th minute, but Morato equalised three minutes later with a header following a set-piece. [[César Palacios (footballer, born 2004)|César Palacios]] restored the visitors’ advantage in the 37th minute before [[Pablo (footballer, born 2004)|Pablo]]<nowiki/>scored his first West Ham goal during first-half stoppage time, leaving the teams level at 2–2 at the interval. <ref>{{Cite web |title=West Ham 2-3 Fulham: Super-sub Oscar Bobb the difference-maker as Alvaro Arbeloa's side edge thrilling London derby |url=https://www.skysports.com/football/west-ham-united-vs-fulham/report/577587 |access-date=2026-09-24 |website=Sky Sports |language=en}}</ref> Substitute [[Oscar Bobb]] scored the decisive goal in the 78th minute, giving Fulham a 3–2 victory and eliminating West Ham from the League Cup. The Hammers came close to another equaliser when [[Timothy Castagne]] cleared Castellanos’ late effort from the goal line. Before the match, West Ham held a period of appreciation for former legendary goalkeeper [[Luděk Mikloško]], who had died following a battle with cancer.<ref>{{Cite web |title=West Ham 2-3 Fulham: Super-sub Oscar Bobb the difference-maker as Alvaro Arbeloa's side edge thrilling London derby |url=https://www.skysports.com/football/west-ham-vs-fulham/report/577587 |access-date=2026-09-24 |website=Sky Sports |language=en}}</ref>

West Ham travelled to [[The Den]] to face rivals [[Millwall F.C.|Millwall]] in the Championship on 19 September. It was the 100th competitive fixture between the clubs and their first meeting since 2012 when West Ham won 2–1, also in the Championship. Millwall took the lead in the 16th minute when [[Caleb Taylor (footballer)|Caleb Taylor]] scored from close range after [[Mads Hermansen]] had saved [[Mark Sykes (footballer)|Mark Sykes’]] initial effort. [[Camiel Neghli]] doubled the home side’s advantage in first-half stoppage time following another long throw from [[Lyndon Dykes]]. <ref>{{Cite web |title=Match Report {{!}} West Ham's second-half comeback secures draw at Millwall - West Ham United FC |url=https://www.whufc.com/en/news/match-report-or-west-hams-second-half-comeback-secures-draw-at-millwall |access-date=2026-09-24 |website=www.whufc.com |language=en}}</ref> <ref name=":6">{{Cite web |last=Sims |first=Andy |last2=read |first2=Press Association·3 min |date=2026-09-19 |title=West Ham come from two goals down to snatch draw at rivals Millwall |url=https://uk.sports.yahoo.com/news/west-ham-come-two-goals-133432195.html |access-date=2026-09-24 |website=Yahoo Sports |language=en-US}}</ref> West Ham improved after introducing Kanté and Pablo at half-time. Kanté reduced the deficit with a low 25-yard strike in the 74th minute, before Mavropanos headed Solomon’s cross into the net six minutes later to complete the comeback. Kanté was subsequently sent off in the 89th minute after bringing down [[Kyrell Lisbie]], but the ten-man Hammers held on for a 2–2 draw.<ref name=":6" />

West Ham went into the 2-week international break in 2nd position in the Championship table, above [[Middlesbrough F.C.|Middlesbrough]] on [[goal difference]] and two points of 1st place [[Swansea City A.F.C.|Swansea City.]]<ref>{{Cite web |title=Championship Table and Standings - Football |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/championship/table |access-date=2026-10-09 |website=BBC Sport |language=en-GB}}</ref>
