'''Bryan Tetsadong Marceau Mbeumo''' ({{IPA|fr|mbø.mo}};<ref>{{Cite web |last=Sulaiman |first=Munir |date=21 November 2023 |title=How to say Premier League players' names correctly |url=https://www.premierleague.com/news/3740592 |access-date=22 November 2023 |publisher=Premier League |archive-date=21 November 2023 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20231121164827/https://www.premierleague.com/news/3740592 |url-status=live }}</ref> born 7 August 1999) is a professional [[Association football|footballer]] who plays primarily as a [[right winger]] for {{English football updater|ManchesU}} club [[Manchester United F.C.|Manchester United]]. Born in France, he represents the [[Cameroon national football team|Cameroon national team]]. He is also capable of playing as a [[Forward (association football)#Striker|striker]] or an [[attacking midfielder]] on occasion.

Mbeumo trained in the [[ES Troyes AC|Troyes]] academy, and began his senior career there in 2016. He was in the club's reserve team initially. He transferred to [[Brentford F.C.|Brentford]] in 2019, and was a member of their squad which gained promotion to the Premier League in 2021. After making 242 appearances for Brentford over six seasons, Mbeumo joined Manchester United in July 2025.

Although he had been capped by France at youth international level, Mbeumo decided to represent Cameroon. He made his senior international debut for them in 2022. At the end of that year, he was included in Cameroon's [[2022 FIFA World Cup|FIFA World Cup]] squad, playing in three matches.

==Club career==
===Troyes===
A left-footed [[Winger (association football)|winger]] with a preference for playing on the right,<ref name="Harris">{{Cite news |last=Harris |first=Jay |title=Bryan Mbeumo's evolution into Brentford's goalscorer |url=https://www.nytimes.com/athletic/4833733/2023/09/06/bryan-mbeumo-goals-brentford/ |url-access=subscription |access-date=7 September 2023 |website=The Athletic|date=6 September 2023 }}</ref> Mbeumo joined [[ES Troyes AC|Troyes]] at the age of 14. He graduated to the club's [[reserve team]] in the [[2016–17 Championnat de France Amateur 2|2016–17]] season.<ref name="Soccerway" /><ref name="JoinsBrentford">{{Cite web |title=Brentford sign Bryan Mbeumo from Troyes |url=https://www.brentfordfc.com/news/2019/august/brentford-sign-bryan-mbeumo-from-troyes/ |access-date=5 August 2019 |publisher=Brentford F.C. |archive-date=11 December 2019 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20191211160957/https://www.brentfordfc.com/news/2019/august/brentford-sign-bryan-mbeumo-from-troyes/ |url-status=live }}</ref> He was in the Troyes U-19 team which won the 2018 [[Coupe Gambardella]], scoring twice in the final at the [[Stade de France]].<ref name="GetToKnow">{{Cite web |title=Get to Know: Bryan Mbeumo |url=https://www.brentfordfc.com/news/2019/august/bryan-muembo-10-things/ |access-date=7 August 2019 |publisher=Brentford F.C. |archive-date=23 September 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200923232250/https://www.brentfordfc.com/news/2019/august/bryan-muembo-10-things/ |url-status=live }}</ref>

Mbeumo made his senior debut for the club in a 1–0 [[Ligue 1]] win over [[FC Metz|Metz]] on 17 February 2018, and made three further appearances during the [[2017–18 Ligue 1|2017–18]] season,<ref name="Soccerway" /> which culminated in Troyes' relegation to [[Ligue 2]].<ref name="GetToKnow" /> Mbeumo became a first-team regular in the [[2018–19 Ligue 2|2018–19]] season, making 40 appearances and scoring 11 goals.<ref name="Soccerway" /> Troyes reached the Ligue 2 promotion play-off semi-finals.<ref name="GetToKnow" /> He departed the [[Stade de l'Aube]] in August 2019, having made 46 senior appearances, and scoring 12 goals.<ref name="Soccerway" /><ref name="JoinsBrentford" />

===Brentford===
====2019–2021====
On 5 August 2019, Mbeumo moved to England to join [[EFL Championship|Championship]] club [[Brentford F.C.|Brentford]] on a five-year contract, for a club record fee of £5.8&nbsp;million.<ref name="JoinsBrentford" /><ref>{{Cite web |date=24 July 2020 |title=Brentford close on Premier League dream – just don't call it 'Moneyball' |url=https://www.espn.co.uk/football/english-league-championship/story/4145973/brentford-close-in-on-premier-league-dream-just-dont-thank-moneyball-for-their-success |access-date=20 August 2020 |publisher=ESPN}}</ref> He made 47 appearances and scored 16 goals in the [[2019–20 Brentford F.C. season|2019–20]] season.<ref name="Soccerbase1920" /> Brentford reached the [[2020 EFL Championship play-off final|2020 Championship play-off final]], but were defeated 2–1 by [[Fulham F.C.|Fulham]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Brentford 1 Fulham 2 |url=https://www.brentfordfc.com/news/2020/august/match-report-for-brentford-vs-fulham-on-04-aug-20/ |access-date=4 August 2020 |publisher=Brentford F.C. |archive-date=7 August 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200807125319/https://www.brentfordfc.com/news/2020/august/match-report-for-brentford-vs-fulham-on-04-aug-20/ |url-status=live }}</ref> Mbeumo's performances were recognised with a nomination for the [[English Football League|EFL]] Player of the Year and Young Player of the Year awards at the 2020 London Football Awards.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Bees up for London Football Awards prizes |url=https://www.brentfordfc.com/news/2020/february/london-football-award-nominations/ |access-date=9 February 2020 |publisher=Brentford F.C. |archive-date=2 May 2022 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220502100202/https://www.brentfordfc.com/news/2020/february/london-football-award-nominations/ |url-status=live }}</ref>

Mbeumo's goalscoring dropped off during the "difficult" [[Impact of the COVID-19 pandemic on association football#Europe|COVID-19]]-affected [[2020–21 Brentford F.C. season|2020–21]] season.<ref>{{Cite web |date=7 March 2021 |title=Mbeumo: This season has been so tough |url=https://www.theleaguepaper.com/latest-news/football-league-championship/372250/brentford-bryan-mbeumo-its-been-so-tough/ |url-access=subscription |access-date=26 April 2023 |website=The League Paper |archive-date=15 April 2021 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210415055448/https://www.theleaguepaper.com/latest-news/football-league-championship/372250/brentford-bryan-mbeumo-its-been-so-tough/ |url-status=live }}</ref> By March 2021, however, his ten [[Assist (association football)|assists]] and four goals helped him gain another nomination for the EFL Player of the Year award, at the 2021 London Football Awards.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Thomas, Ivan, and Bryan shortlisted for London Football Awards |url=https://www.brentfordfc.com/news/2021/march/three-bees-nominated-at-london-football-awards/ |access-date=22 March 2021 |publisher=Brentford F.C. |archive-date=18 May 2021 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210518015213/https://www.brentfordfc.com/news/2021/march/three-bees-nominated-at-london-football-awards/ |url-status=live }}</ref> Mbeumo finished the season with eight goals in 49 appearances, and Brentford qualified for the [[Premier League]] by winning the [[2021 EFL Championship play-off final|2021 Championship play-off final]], in which they defeated [[Swansea City]] 2–0.<ref name="Soccerbase2021" /><ref name="Vincent" />

Men&#x27;s Team {{!}} Manchester United |url=https://www.manutd.com/en/teams/mens-team/bryan-mbeumo |access-date=30 August 2026 |website=Manchester United}}</ref>On 17 August, he made his debut for the club in a 1–0 home defeat to [[Arsenal F.C.–Manchester United F.C. rivalry|rivals]] [[Arsenal F.C.|Arsenal]] in the league.<ref>{{Cite web |last1=Jackson |first1=Jamie |date=17 August 2025 |title=Calafiori strikes after goalkeeper's error as Arsenal grind to win at Manchester United |work=The Guardian |url=https://www.theguardian.com/football/2025/aug/17/manchester-united-arsenal-premier-league-match-report |access-date=23 August 2025 }}</ref> He scored his first goal for the club in a 2–2 away draw against [[EFL League Two]] side [[Grimsby Town F.C.|Grimsby Town]] in the [[EFL Cup]] [[2025–26 EFL Cup#Third round|third round]], before missing the decisive penalty as United lost the shootout 11–12.<ref>{{cite news |last=Stone |first=Simon |date=27 August 2025 |title=Grimsby 2-2 Manchester United (12-11 pens) |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/football/live/c5y387ngzz4t |access-date=27 August 2025 |work=[[BBC Sport]] |publisher=[[BBC]]}}</ref> Mbeumo went on to score his first Premier League goal for United in the following match, a 3–2 win against [[Burnley F.C.|Burnley]] at [[Old Trafford]].<ref>{{cite news |date=31 July 2025 |title="Manchester United vs Burnley" |url=https://www.premierleague.com/en/match/2561920/manchester-united-vs-burnley?tab=recap |access-date=31 July 2025 |work=[[Premier League]] |department=[[Premier League]] |publisher=[[Premier League]]}}</ref>

On 19 October 2025, Mbeumo scored the opening goal against rivals [[Liverpool F.C.|Liverpool]] after only one minute, helping United eventually win 2–1, their first victory at [[Anfield]] since 2016.<ref>{{cite news |date=20 October 2025 |title="Report: Liverpool 1 United 2" |url=https://www.manutd.com/en/news/detail/match-report-from-liverpool-vs-man-utd-in-premier-league-19-october-2025 |access-date=22 October 2025 |work=[[Manchester United]]}}</ref> The next week, Mbeumo scored a brace against [[Brighton & Hove Albion F.C.|Brighton & Hove Albion]], securing a 4–2 victory for United.<ref>{{Cite web |last= |first= |date=25 October 2025 |title=Bryan Mbeumo names Man United change that has sparked stunning turnaround |url=https://sports.yahoo.com/article/bryan-mbeumo-names-man-united-213000438.html |access-date=26 October 2025 |website=Yahoo Sports |language=en-US}}</ref> His performances in October, which also included an assist for [[Mason Mount]] against [[Sunderland A.F.C.|Sunderland]], earned him his first [[Premier League Player of the Month]] award.<ref>{{Cite web |date=7 November 2025 |title=Mbeumo wins his first EA Sports Player of the Month award |url=https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4455047/mbeumo-wins-his-first-ever-ea-sports-player-of-the-month-award |access-date=7 November 2025 |website=Premier League |language=en-UK}}</ref>

On 17 January 2026, Mbeumo played his first game back from the [[2025 Africa Cup of Nations|Africa Cup of Nations]], starting in the [[Manchester derby|derby]] against [[Manchester City F.C.|Manchester City]] at Old Trafford. He scored the opening goal, his first ever against City, with United winning 2–0.<ref>{{Cite web |date=17 January 2026 |title=Report: Manchester United 2 City 0 |url=https://www.manutd.com/en/news/detail/match-report-man-utd-v-man-city-premier-league-17-january-2026 |access-date=18 January 2026 |website=Manchester United |language=en-UK}}</ref> On 25 January, Mbeumo scored against rivals Arsenal away which contributed to United winning 3–2.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Premier League (Sky Sports) |url=https://www.skysports.com/football/arsenal-vs-manchester-united/video/531349 |access-date=25 January 2026 |website=Sky Sports |language=en}}</ref> He finished his debut season as the club's joint top scorer with 11 league goals and 12 goals in all competitions, level with [[Benjamin Šeško]].<ref>{{cite web |url=https://foot-africa.com/en/news/premier-league-bryan-mbeumo-finishes-as-manchester-uniteds-top-scorer-1218056/ |title=Premier League: Bryan Mbeumo finishes as Manchester United's top scorer |publisher=Foot Africa |access-date=24 May 2026 }}</ref>

==International career==
[[File:Bryan Mbeumo.jpg|thumb|Mbeumo lining up for [[France national under-20 football team|France U20]] in 2019|250x250px]]
Mbeumo won 10 caps and scored one goal for France at [[France national under-17 football team|U17]], [[France national under-20 football team|U20]] and [[France national under-21 football team|U21]] level.<ref>{{FFF male player|2543851605<!-- -bryan-mbeumo -->}}</ref> In August 2022, following a meeting in London with [[Samuel Eto'o]], Mbeumo declared his senior international allegiance to the [[Cameroon national football team|Cameroon national team]].<ref name="2022WCSquad">{{Cite web |title=Mbeumo heading to Qatar |url=https://www.brentfordfc.com/en/news/article/bryan-mbeumo-in-cameroon-squad-for-2022-world-cup |access-date=10 November 2022 |website=brentfordfc.com |archive-date=12 November 2022 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20221112011920/https://www.brentfordfc.com/en/news/article/bryan-mbeumo-in-cameroon-squad-for-2022-world-cup |url-status=live }}</ref> He made 9 appearances and scored one goal during the 2022–23 season, with three appearances coming during Cameroon's [[2022 FIFA World Cup|2022 World Cup]] campaign,<ref name="NFT">{{NFT player|88687|access-date=16 September 2025}}</ref> prior to the team's group stage exit.<ref>{{Cite news |title=Cameroon beat Brazil but knocked out of World Cup |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/football/63752145 |access-date=11 June 2023 |work=BBC Sport |archive-date=26 September 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240926112433/https://www.bbc.com/sport/football/63752145 |url-status=live }}</ref> After appearing in three of Cameroon's four [[2023 Africa Cup of Nations qualification Group C|2023 Africa Cup of Nations qualifying]] matches and scoring one goal,<ref name="NFT" /> injury ruled Mbeumo out of the [[2023 Africa Cup of Nations|final tournament]].<ref name="Operation" />

Mbeumo was included in the Cameroon national team squad selected by head coach [[David Pagou]] for the [[2025 Africa Cup of Nations]], held in [[Morocco]].<ref>{{cite web|access-date=1 December 2025 |language=fr |title=André Onana et Vincent Aboubakar absents, Christian Kofane et Eric-Junior Dina Ebimbe appelés... La liste du Cameroun pour la CAN 2025 |url=https://www.lequipe.fr/Football/Actualites/Andre-onana-et-vincent-aboubakar-absents-christian-kofane-et-eric-junior-dina-ebimbe-appeles-la-liste-du-cameroun-pour-la-can-2025/1613836 |website=L'Équipe}}<!-- auto-translated from French by Module:CS1 translator --></ref><ref name="PLAFCON2025">{{cite web|access-date=2026-10-20 |date=2025-12-12 |title=Squads announced: Which Premier League stars are going to AFCON 2025? |url=https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4499030 |website=Premier League}}<!-- auto-translated from French by Module:CS1 translator --></ref> He recorded one assist in the group-stage match against [[Gabon national football team|Gabon]], contributing to Cameroon's 1–0 victory, and featured in a 1–1 draw against [[Ivory Coast national football team|Ivory Coast]]. He was substituted in the second half of the match against [[Mozambique national football team|Mozambique]], as Cameroon won 2–1 to advance from the group stage. Mbeumo also featured in the round of 16 match against [[South Africa national football team|South Africa]], helping his team secure a 2–1 victory. However, Cameroon were eliminated in the quarter-finals after losing 2–0 to hosts [[Morocco national football team|Morocco]]. Mbeumo made a total of five appearances and recorded one assist in the tournament.<ref name="FBrefAFCON2025">{{cite web|access-date=2026-10-20 |title=Africa Cup of Nations Bryan Mbeumo Match Logs |url=https://fbref.com/en/players/6afaebf2/matchlogs/c656/summary/Bryan-Mbeumo-Match-Logs |website=FBref}}<!-- auto-translated from French by Module:CS1 translator --></ref>

==Style of play==
Mbeumo is a versatile forward who primarily operates as a [[right winger|right winger]] in a front three, often cutting inside onto his stronger left foot. He can also be played as a [[Forward (association football)|striker]] or [[Attacking Midfielder|attacking midfielder]]. In systems using a [[Formation (association football)|3–4–2–1]] formation, he typically plays as an [[inside forward]] in the right half-space, where he can exploit defensive gaps, link up with teammates, and pose a threat on goal.<ref name="PL20250721">{{cite web |last=Keble |first=Alex |title=Analysis: Mbeumo the perfect fit for Amorim's new plan |url=https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4323193/analysis-mbeumo-the-perfect-fit-for-amorims-new-plan |website=Premier League |date=21 July 2025 |access-date=7 August 2025}}</ref>

Known for his pace, direct [[dribbling]], and movement off the ball, Mbeumo excels in quick attacking transitions. He is particularly effective at carrying the ball into dangerous areas and creating high-quality chances, as reflected by his league-leading [[Assist (association football)#Statistics|expected assists]] (xA) in the [[2024–25 Premier League|2024–25 season]].<ref name="PL20250721"/> Despite not being a traditional striker, he also recorded the highest number of non-penalty goals over expected (npxG overperformance) during the same campaign, showcasing his clinical finishing.<ref name="PL20250721"/>
