Zion Suzuki (Aston Villa): Fact, he's just a big-game player who gets the job done, comes up with big saves. He couldn't do anything about Erling Haaland's goal.

Matty Cash (Aston Villa): It was like a captain's performance from the right-back. A lot of people go up against Jeremy Doku and struggle. He didn't. He gets the job done. I thought it was really excellent and epitomised everything Aston Villa were about. If Man City did get down his side, he recovered, went again. I just thought it was an exceptional display from him.
James Hill (Bournemouth) and Ezri Konsa (Arsenal): We're going to have Konsa and Hill. Both of these two are in for the same reason: they're absolutely solid. They just do their job effectively well. No-one's talking about them for their theatrics.
No-one's talking about them because they play out well from the back. They're just proper defenders who defend. Obviously Konsa gets a goal, so he gets an extra little part for that, but Hill was celebrating making tackles because that's what he does.
Adrien Truffert (Bournemouth): People will be talking about Manchester United and how poor they are, and is the manager going to get sacked and all of that stuff. However, if you keep playing the way that Bournemouth are, with the attacking intent, and obviously you've got a left-back who's giving you assists and shutting down people defensively, I think he deserve a little bit of credit. So, he comes into the team.

Shea Charles (Fulham): I didn't really want to put a Fulham player in because I thought these are games they should be winning. However, the fact that they've won at Coventry and they got a clean sheet, and Charles has been excellent since he's come from Southampton, I just thought he should go in.
He controlled the game and adds a spark to a Fulham team that is pretty weak - lacklustre is the word I would use at times. Since he's been in the team regularly, guess what, they've started to pick up some results. So, fair play to them.
Nico Gonzalez (Newcastle): Gonzalez might be the best signing that Newcastle have made, because they've got all these young players that have got pace, speed, good in the wide areas. The manager wants it to be like the the Red Bull [Salzburg] team of old, which is sprinting more and all of that fun stuff. You need someone that can just control the game, one and two touch, and Nico Gonzalez absolutely does that. I thought he was exceptional.
Marcus Tavernier (Bournemouth): We're going to force Tavernier in as the 10. Not only for this game, but if you're Bournemouth, before last weekend when they beat Sunderland, we were talking about how they haven't got any wins. They're struggling in the league, what about Europa League? Is it too much?
And in one week, they've beaten Sunderland, AC Milan and Man Utd. They've turned this season from 'maybe' to 'this is unbelievable', and eight goals scored, no goals conceded. Tavernier scored in those games. He scored again against United, gives you a load more just in terms of who and what he is. He's just a really good player.
