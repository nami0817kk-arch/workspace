## 1. 計算式（FIFA 公式の説明 PDF）

- 「Revision of the FIFA/Coca-Cola World Ranking」 https://digitalhub.fifa.com/m/f99da4f73212220/original/edbm045h0udbwkqew35a-pdf.pdf （procedure-men のページ https://inside.fifa.com/fifa-world-ranking/procedure-men から。ページは 2025-03-26 更新）
- 原文:
  - "P = Pbefore + I * (W – We)"
  - "I= 10 Friendly matches played during International Match Calendar windows" / "I= 15 Group phase matches of Nations League competitions" / "I= 25 Qualification matches ..." / "I= 50 FIFA World Cup final competition matches up until QF stage"
  - "W: result of the match 1 = win; 0.5 = draw; 0 = defeat"
  - "We = 1 / (10(-dr/600) + 1)"（dr＝試合前の両チームの点の差）
  - "Matches decided in a PSO are treated as a draw for the losing team and as "half a win" for the winning team: W for the winning team is 0.75 instead of 1.0"
- procedure-men のページの原文: "The points which are added or subtracted are partially determined by the relative strength of the two opponents, including the logical expectation that teams higher in the ranking should fare better against teams lower in the ranking."
