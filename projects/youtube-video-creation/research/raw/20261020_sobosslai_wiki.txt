'''Dominik Szoboszlai''' ({{IPA|hu|ˈsoboslɒi ˈdominik}} {{respell|SO|bo|sloy}};<ref>{{Cite web |last=Price |first=GlennI’m  |date=2 July 2023 |title=How do you pronounce 'Dominik Szoboszlai'? The man himself reveals |url=https://www.liverpoolfc.com/news/how-do-you-pronounce-dominik-szoboszlai-man-himself-reveals |access-date=3 July 2023 |website=[[Liverpool FC]]}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |date=31 July 2020 |title=Szoboszlai Dominik a Csisztu Sport Castben, a teljes interjú! |trans-title=Dominik Szoboszlai in the Csisztu Sport Cast, the full interview! |url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yQyg6v18Xms&t=53s |access-date=12 October 2023 |via=YouTube |language=Hungarian |archive-date=16 November 2023 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20231116210531/https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yQyg6v18Xms&t=53s |url-status=live }}</ref> born 25 October 2000) is a Hungarian professional [[Association football|footballer]] who plays and serves as vice-captain for {{English football updater|Liverpoo}} club [[Liverpool F.C.|Liverpool]] and [[Captain (association football)|captains]] the [[Hungary national football team|Hungary national team]]. A versatile player, Szoboszlai can be deployed as a [[central midfielder|central]] or [[attacking midfielder]], [[wide midfielder]], or [[right-back]].

Coming through the youth system, Szoboszlai made his senior debut in 2017 with Austrian club [[FC Liefering]], the reserve team of [[FC Red Bull Salzburg|Red Bull Salzburg]]. In January 2018, Szoboszlai debuted with the parent club, becoming a starter from the [[2018–19 Austrian Football Bundesliga|2018–19 season]]. Following three seasons, where he helped his club win three [[Austrian Football Bundesliga|league]] titles and two [[Austrian Cup|domestic cups]], in January 2021, Szoboszlai moved to Germany at [[RB Leipzig]], a club associated with Red Bull Salzburg, for a reported fee of €20&nbsp;million, making him the most expensive Hungarian footballer of all time.<ref>{{Cite web |date=19 December 2020 |title=Youngster Szoboszlai Becomes Most Expensive Hungarian Footballer Ever |url=https://hungarytoday.hu/szoboszlai-transfer-expensive-hungarian-footballer-rb-leipzig/ |access-date=6 March 2024 |archive-date=19 December 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201219172551/https://hungarytoday.hu/szoboszlai-transfer-expensive-hungarian-footballer-rb-leipzig/ |url-status=dead }}</ref> In his three seasons at the club he helped his side win two [[DFB-Pokal]] titles. In July 2023, he joined Liverpool after they paid his [[release clause]] of €70&nbsp;million, making him the club's fourth-most expensive signing of all time. Szoboszlai won the [[2023–24 EFL Cup|EFL Cup]] in his first season in England, securing the [[2024–25 Premier League|Premier League]] the following year.

Szoboszlai represented Hungary internationally, both at youth and senior levels. He made his senior debut at the [[UEFA Euro 2020 qualifying Group E|Euro 2020 qualifiers]], helping his country qualify to the [[UEFA Euro 2020|finals]] by scoring in the last minute in the [[UEFA Euro 2020 qualifying play-offs|play-offs]] against [[Iceland national football team|Iceland]].

== Player profile ==
=== Style of play ===
Szoboszlai has been described as a [[box-to-box]] midfielder.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Ajith |first=Shambhu |date=30 September 2023 |title=5 best box-to-box midfielders in the Premier League this season (2023–24) |url=https://www.sportskeeda.com/football/5-best-box-to-box-midfielders-premier-league-season-2023-24-rice-szoboszlai |access-date=27 December 2023 |website=sportskeeda.com |language=en-gb}}</ref> According to Bundesliga expert [[Raphael Honigstein]], "he's a very technical player, very easy on the eye, quite tall as well, so he looks imposing".<ref>{{Cite web |date=July 2023 |title='A great technical attacking midfielder' – the inside track on Dominik Szoboszlai |url=https://www.liverpoolfc.com/news/great-technical-attacking-midfielder-inside-track-dominik-szoboszlai |access-date=27 December 2023 |website=liverpoolfc.com |language=en}}</ref>

In an interview published on [[Nemzeti Sport]], his manager, Mátyás Esterházy, said that Szoboszlai is a versatile footballer since he plays different positions in Liverpool and the national team. While he has a defined role in Liverpool, [[Marco Rossi (footballer, born 1964)|Marco Rossi]] gives more freedom to him in the national team.<ref>{{Cite web |year=2023 |title=Sportrádió: Nekem a legnagyobb élmény idén Schäfer visszatérése – E |url=http://www.nemzetisport.hu/minden_mas_foci/sportradio-nekem-a-legnagyobb-elmeny-iden-schfer-visszaterese-esterhazy-matyas-2995283 |access-date=23 December 2023 |website=nemzetisport.hu |language=hu}}</ref>

In the 2025–26 season, Szoboszlai was noted for his tactical versatility and work rate, having been deployed not only as an attacking midfielder but also in deeper midfield roles and at right-back.<ref name="Athletic2026" /> Writing for ''[[The Athletic]]'', Andy Jones and Gregg Evans highlighted his passing range, pressing intensity and goal contributions, describing him as capable of influencing matches both offensively and defensively.<ref name="Athletic2026" /> According to ''[[BBC Sport]]'', Szoboszlai ranked among Liverpool's most active players in terms of distance covered and sprint statistics during the campaign.<ref>{{cite news |last=Patel |first=Aadam |title=Dominik Szoboszlai: The rise of the midfielder and why he's becoming a leader at Liverpool |url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/football/articles/cjrq74ryrx7o
 |work=[[BBC Sport]] |date=19 February 2026 |access-date=}}</ref>

=== Reception ===
Szoboszlai is often referred to as Hungary's leading footballer.<ref name="ReceptionHungary">{{Citation |title= Dominik Szoboszlai: The rise of Liverpool's Hungarian hero |work= BBC Sport |date= 6 December 2023 |url= https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/67508072 |access-date=9 December 2023 |language=en}}</ref> He has frequently been compared to compatriot [[Ferenc Puskás]], with Hungarian football pundit Ábel Mészáros saying: "Szoboszlai is ahead of everyone since [[Lajos Détári]]. You really have to go far back… He is approaching the realm of Puskás and others in that class."<ref name="ReceptionHungary"/>

In 2023, Szoboszlai was listed as the 52nd best footballer in the world by [[The Guardian]].<ref>{{cite web |title=The 100 best male footballers in the world 2023 |url=https://www.theguardian.com/football/ng-interactive/2023/dec/19/the-100-best-male-footballers-in-the-world-2023
 |website=The Guardian |date=21 December 2023 |access-date=}}</ref> In the same year, he was also included in [[FourFourTwo]]’s list of the 100 Best Football Players in the World 2023.<ref>{{cite web |title=Ranked! The 100 best football players in the world 2023 |url=https://www.fourfourtwo.com/features/ranked-the-100-best-football-players-in-the-world-2023
 |website=FourFourTwo |access-date=}}</ref>

Following Liverpool’s 3–0 victory over Brighton & Hove Albion in the [[FA Cup]] fourth round on 14 February 2026, [[Mohamed Salah]] described Szoboszlai as “one of the best players in the world right now”.<ref name="Athletic2026">{{cite news |last1=Jones |first1=Andy |last2=Evans |first2=Gregg |title=How Dominik Szoboszlai became ‘one of the best players in the world’ |url=https://www.nytimes.com/athletic/7054674/2026/02/19/dominik-szoboszlai-liverpool-best-player-world/
 |work=[[The Athletic]] |publisher=The New York Times |date=19 February 2026 |access-date=}}</ref> In the same in-depth analysis for ''[[The Athletic]]'', Andy Jones and Gregg Evans highlighted Szoboszlai's versatility, goal contributions, passing range and leadership, concluding that his performances during the season had earned him a place in discussions about belonging among the best players in the world.<ref name="Athletic2026" /> In a ''[[BBC Sport]]'' article, Aadam Patel described him as Liverpool's “key man”.<ref>{{cite news |last=Patel |first=Aadam |title=How Szoboszlai became Liverpool's key man
|url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/football/articles/cjrq74ryrx7o
