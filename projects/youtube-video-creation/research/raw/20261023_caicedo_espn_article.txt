Moisés Caicedo is determined to repay the faith shown in him by Chelsea boss Xabi Alonso and become more of a leader on the pitch.
Caicedo started his first game under Alonso following a spell out injured in Saturday's thumping 5-1 win over Bournemouth.
The 24-year-old midfielder is a member of the leadership group at the Blues' Cobham training ground and knows he needs to make himself heard on the pitch.
Moisés Caicedo has vowed to repay the faith put in him by Chelsea manager Xabi Alonso. Pedro Porru/MB Media/Getty Images"With Xabi I am going to keep developing, for sure, and show him why he trusts me," Caicedo told the club website.
"I think every coach tells me that I can improve talking on the pitch, you know? Because they know that I'm a leader, but it will be better for me if I start talking more with the players.
"I try my best, but it's not in my nature. So when I'm not able to talk, I just try to show even more.
