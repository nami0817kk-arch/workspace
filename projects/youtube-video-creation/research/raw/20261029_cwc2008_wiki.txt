The '''2008 FIFA Club World Cup final''' was the final match of the [[2008 FIFA Club World Cup]], an [[association football]] tournament for the champion clubs from each of [[FIFA]]'s six continental confederations. The match took place at the [[International Stadium Yokohama]], Japan, on 21 December 2008, and pitted [[LDU Quito]] of Ecuador, the [[CONMEBOL]] club champions, and [[Manchester United F.C.|Manchester United]] of England, the [[UEFA]] club champions. Despite going down to ten men early in the second half, Manchester United won the match 1–0 thanks to a 73rd-minute goal from [[Wayne Rooney]].<ref>{{cite news|url=http://news.bbc.co.uk/sport2/hi/football/teams/m/man_utd/7793438.stm|title=Liga de Quito 0–1 Man Utd |date=21 December 2008|work=BBC Sport|access-date=22 September 2009}}</ref>

==Road to final==
Both LDU Quito and Manchester United qualified for the competition at the semi-final stage, after winning their respective continental competition, the [[2008 Copa Libertadores]] and the [[2007–08 UEFA Champions League]].

LDU Quito's opening game was against [[Federación Mexicana de Fútbol Asociación|Mexican]] side [[C.F. Pachuca|Pachuca]], who had defeated [[Egyptian Football Association|Egyptian]] club [[Al Ahly SC|Al Ahly]] 4–2 in the quarter-finals after [[extra time]].<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.fifa.com/clubworldcup/matches/round=252217/match=300073123/report.html |title=Al-Ahly – Pachuca Match Report |publisher=[[FIFA]] |date=13 December 2008 |access-date=20 December 2008 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090216225713/http://www.fifa.com/clubworldcup/matches/round=252217/match=300073123/report.html |archive-date=16 February 2009 |url-status=dead }}</ref> Their semi-final match was played at the [[Olympic Stadium (Tokyo)|National Stadium]] in Tokyo on 17 December 2008.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.fifa.com/clubworldcup/matches/round=252222/match=300073125/report.html |title=Pachuca – Liga de Quito Match Report |publisher=[[FIFA]] |date=17 December 2008 |access-date=20 December 2008 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090202043904/http://www.fifa.com/clubworldcup/matches/round=252222/match=300073125/report.html |archive-date=2 February 2009 |url-status=dead }}</ref> The Ecuadorian side scored two goals in the first 26&nbsp;minutes through [[Claudio Bieler]] and [[Luis Bolaños]], securing their place in the Club World Cup final.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.fifa.com/clubworldcup/matches/round=252222/match=300073125/summary.html |title=In a Liga of their own |publisher=[[FIFA]] |date=17 December 2008 |access-date=20 December 2008 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20081221083457/http://www.fifa.com/clubworldcup/matches/round%3D252222/match%3D300073125/summary.html |archive-date=21 December 2008 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

Manchester United's semi-final opponents were [[Gamba Osaka]], who had previously defeated [[Football Federation Australia|Australian]] [[A-League]] premiers and [[2008 AFC Champions League|Asian Champions League]] runners-up [[Adelaide United FC|Adelaide United]] (1–0) to move into the second semi-final on 18 December.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.fifa.com/clubworldcup/matches/round=252217/match=300073122/report.html |title=Adelaide United – Gamba Osaka Match Report |publisher=[[FIFA]] |date=14 December 2008 |access-date=20 December 2008 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090130072925/http://www.fifa.com/clubworldcup/matches/round=252217/match=300073122/report.html |archive-date=30 January 2009 |url-status=dead }}</ref> [[Nemanja Vidić]] and [[Cristiano Ronaldo]] opened the scoring in the first half, building up a two-goal lead at half-time. [[Masato Yamazaki (footballer, born 1981)|Masato Yamazaki]] was the next to score in the 74th minute to bring Gamba back into contention, but [[Wayne Rooney]] scored twice and [[Darren Fletcher]] once within the next five minutes to extend United's lead. [[Yasuhito Endō]] converted an 85th-minute [[Penalty kick (association football)|penalty kick]] and [[Hideo Hashimoto]] got a third for Gamba just minutes before the final whistle. The result was 5–3, taking Manchester United to the final.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.fifa.com/clubworldcup/matches/round=252222/match=300073124/summary.html |title=United hit five in thriller |publisher=[[FIFA]] |date=18 December 2008 |access-date=20 December 2008 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090122065019/http://www.fifa.com/clubworldcup/matches/round%3D252222/match%3D300073124/summary.html |archive-date=22 January 2009 |url-status=dead }}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.fifa.com/clubworldcup/matches/round=252222/match=300073124/report.html |title=Gamba Osaka – Manchester United Match Report |publisher=[[FIFA]] |date=18 December 2008 |access-date=20 December 2008 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090202051203/http://www.fifa.com/clubworldcup/matches/round=252222/match=300073124/report.html |archive-date=2 February 2009 |url-status=dead }}</ref>
