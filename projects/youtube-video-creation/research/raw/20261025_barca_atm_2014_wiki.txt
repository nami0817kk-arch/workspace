The '''Barcelona vs Atlético Madrid''' [[Association football|football]] match that took place on 17 May 2014 at the [[Camp Nou]] in [[Barcelona]], [[Spain]], was played during the 38th and therefore last round of the [[2013–14 La Liga]] season. Atlético and Barcelona came into the match occupying the top two positions in the league, separated by just three points, and thus both teams could still claim the title.

It was the first time since 1951 and just the third time in league history the [[La Liga]] title came down to a head-to-head match on the final weekend of play (the others in [[1945–46 La Liga|1945–46]] and [[1950–51 La Liga|1950–51]] each involved one of the clubs involved in 2014, with [[Sevilla FC|Sevilla]] the other club on both occasions).<ref name="First time">{{cite news|title=Last day drama in Spain|publisher=ESPN|author=Michael Yokhin|date=16 May 2014|url=https://www.espnfc.com/blog/_/name/laliga/id/1879?cc=5901|access-date=21 April 2021|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140520075734/https://www.espnfc.com/blog/_/name/laliga/id/1879|archive-date=20 May 2014}}</ref> The result was a 1–1 draw, earning Atlético Madrid the point to win their first league title in 18 years, and their tenth overall.

==Background==
In the 36th round of La Liga, Atlético lost 0–2 to [[Levante UD|Levante]] at [[Estadi Ciutat de València]] while Barcelona and Real Madrid only earned one point in their home games against [[Getafe CF|Getafe]] and [[Valencia CF|Valencia]] respectively. With two weeks to play, Atlético led the table with a 3-point advantage over Barcelona and one point more over [[Real Madrid C.F.|Real Madrid]].

After these results, Atlético could have won the league in the 37th and penultimate round if they earned one more point than Barcelona and Real. In this round, Atlético had a home tie against [[Málaga CF|Málaga]] while Barcelona visited relegation-threatened [[Elche CF|Elche]] and Real Madrid played at [[Balaídos]] against [[Celta de Vigo|Celta Vigo]].

Despite both Barcelona and Real Madrid failing to win their respective games (Barcelona ended 0–0<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.sbnation.com/soccer/2014/5/11/5707372/barcelona-elche-2014-la-liga-final-score-result|title=Elche vs. Barcelona: Final score 0-0, but Blaugrana alive despite dropped points|last=McCauley|first=Kim|publisher=SBNation|date=11 May 2014|access-date=19 February 2026}}</ref> and Real were out of the title race after losing 2–0<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.betnumbers.gr/index.php?option=com_k2&view=item&id=256:celta-vigo-2-0-real-madrid-charles-sonroja-a-un-indolente-real-madrid&Itemid=730|title=Celta Vigo 2-0 Real Madrid: Charles double ends Ancelotti's men's title hopes|publisher=BetNumbers.gr|date=11 May 2014|access-date=28 July 2016}}{{Dead link|date=October 2019|bot=InternetArchiveBot|fix-attempted=yes}}</ref>), Atlético could not win the title at home as they only could earn one point after a 1–1 draw.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.bbc.com/sport/football/27301114|publisher=[[BBC Sport]]|title=Atletico Madrid 1-1 Malaga|date=11 May 2014|access-date=28 July 2016}}</ref>

These results meant that the title would be decided in a head-to-head game at [[Camp Nou]] between Barcelona and Atlético, with Atlético having the advantage with a 3-point lead.<ref name="First time"/> The reverse fixture ended 0–0 meaning Atlético needed to avoid defeat to clinch the title, otherwise Barcelona would successfully defend their [[2012–13 La Liga|La Liga crown]] due to having the better head-to-head record out of the two sides.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.independent.co.uk/sport/football/european/la-liga-barcelona-v-atletico-madrid-match-preview-9385691.html|work=[[The Independent]]|last=Rice|first=Simon|title=La Liga: Barcelona v Atletico Madrid match preview|date=16 May 2014|access-date=19 February 2026}}</ref>

==Match==
Atlético Madrid forwards [[Diego Costa]] and [[Arda Turan]] became injured within the first 30 minutes, and were substituted off. Five minutes before half time, Barcelona took the lead into the break thanks to a goal by [[Alexis Sánchez]] after receiving a chest-pass from [[Lionel Messi]].

In the 49th minute of the second half however, a header from [[Diego Godín]] off a corner kick made the score 1–1. Eight minutes after the goal, Barcelona's [[Sergio Busquets]] could not continue playing.

In the 64th minute Messi scored, but Barcelona were denied the lead they needed as the goal was ruled out for offside. Eventually the game ended as a 1–1 draw, which handed the ''Colchoneros'' their first league title in 18 years, and their 10th overall.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.theguardian.com/football/2014/may/17/barcelona-atletico-madrid-la-liga|title=Atlético Madrid celebrate La Liga title after draw at Barcelona|last=Lowe|first=Sid|work=[[The Guardian]]|date=17 May 2014|access-date=19 February 2026}}</ref>

It was the first time since the [[2003–04 La Liga]] that a club other than Barcelona or [[Real Madrid C.F.|Real Madrid]] won the title. It was also the first time in the 67-year history of the [[Camp Nou]] stadium that a visiting team had clinched the title in the stadium.

The draw meant that Barcelona would finish second, regardless of Real Madrid's result against Barcelona's [[derbi barceloní|city rivals]] [[RCD Espanyol|Espanyol]], as ''Barça'' would at worst have the better head-to-head record over their ''[[El Clásico|Clásico]]'' rivals (winning 2–1 at home and 4–3 away). Nonetheless, Real Madrid won 3–1, meaning second place was instead decided on the head-to-head tiebreaker.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.skysports.com/football/r-madrid-vs-espanyol/311652|title=Primera Liga report: Real Madrid 3 Espanyol 1|publisher=British Sky Broadcasting|date=17 May 2014|access-date=18 September 2017}}</ref>

Just after the game, [[Gerardo Martino]] resigned as Barcelona's manager after one season in charge. The club only won the [[2013 Supercopa de España|Supercopa de España]], they were eliminated by Atlético Madrid in the quarter-finals of the [[2013–14 UEFA Champions League|Champions League]] and lost 2–1 against Real Madrid in the [[2014 Copa del Rey Final|Copa del Rey Final]]. Two days later, [[Diego Godín]] was named [[La Liga Player of the Month|Player of the Month]] for May.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.laliga.com/noticias/premios-bbva-a-los-mejores-de-mayo|title=Premios BBVA a los mejores de mayo|publisher=LFP.es|date=19 May 2014|access-date=19 February 2026|language=es}}</ref>

After celebrating the league title in [[Madrid]], Atlético played in the [[2014 UEFA Champions League Final|Champions League Final]] seven days later, against their [[Madrid Derby|local rivals]] Real Madrid. Despite a header from [[Diego Godín]] in the first half of the game, [[Sergio Ramos]] equalised for Real Madrid in the 3rd minute of second half stoppage time.<ref>{{cite news|title=Real finally secure La Decima|url=https://www.espnfc.com/us/en/report/392450/report.html?soccernet=true&cc=5901|access-date=25 May 2014|newspaper=ESPN FC|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140526040156/https://www.espnfc.com/us/en/report/392450/report.html?soccernet=true&cc=5901|archive-date=26 May 2014|url-status=dead}}</ref> In extra time, the Atlético players were visibly exhausted and eventually lost 4–1.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/27383593|title=Real Madrid 4-1 Atletico Madrid|publisher=BBC Sport|last=McNulty|first=Phil|date=25 May 2014|access-date=19 February 2026}}</ref>
