Luis Enrique
“It’s a great win. We started the game with the same focus as usual, but we struggled on the counter-attack and that’s how they scored their goal. When you play away from home, your opponents are always more motivated. But in the end, we were the ones who dominated the match. We created lots of chances and saw our players perform at a very high level, so we’re happy. 
We saw what the Titis are capable of, even if it’s difficult to match the standard set by Paris Saint-Germain. But that’s what we’re aiming for: to be as competitive as possible in every match, whoever the opponent. The win record? I’m lucky enough to manage a club of this level and to have incredible players. I’m very happy and I hope to continue making history in Paris.”
Achraf Hakimi
“It was a very good performance from the whole team, we’re very happy. It wasn’t easy, we started by conceding a goal, but we showed our spirit and had the right attitude to get back into the game. That’s what matters most. It’s a great evening for me, especially because we won, but also because it’s my first hat-trick. I’m happy and I hope to score as many goals as possible in this shirt.”
