  West Ham United have unveiled their home kit for the new season, its first with a new manufacturer.
  The 2026/27 kit by New Balance incorporates the club's ‘Bonds Blue’ sleeves.
  This is West Ham's first collaboration with the apparel brand.
Jarrod Bowen in the new home shirt 
(Image: West Ham United)
  The club said the claret body of the shirt features woven chevron detailing, completed with claret and blue patterns on the collar and cuffs.

  Supporters can now opt for the Elite Shirt, which matches the version worn by players on the pitch.
  Both men's and women's team players, including Jarrod Bowen, Tomas Soucek, Freddie Potts, Soungoutou Magassa, Riko Ueki, Leila Wandeler, and Ffion Morgan, modelled the new kit.
  Fans can buy the kit at the club’s stores located at London Stadium, Liberty Romford, Lakeside Thurrock, and Basildon Eastgate or order online at the club's shop website.
