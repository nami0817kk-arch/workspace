Bayern Munich snap Arsenal win streak in thrilling 2-2 draw
2026-10-21T21:04:00Z

Declan Rice
 stole the limelight from 
Harry Kane
 as 
Arsenal
 hit back to snatch a thrilling 2-2 draw against 
Bayern Munich
 in a heavyweight Champions League clash Wednesday at the Allianz Arena.
Gunners midfielder Rice lashed low beyond Bayern goalkeeper 
Manuel Neuer
 eight minutes from time to extend his side's unbeaten start to this season's competition following back-to-back wins over 
Napoli
 and 
Lille
.
The draw did, however, bring an end to Arsenal's 14-game winning streak in the UCL League Phase -- the second longest after Bayern in competition history.
Kane -- Tottenham's all-time record scorer -- was on course to claim the winner after registering his 16th goal in 23 career outings against Arsenal by converting a 61st-minute penalty following Jurrien Timber's handball.
The 
England
 captain, who is contention to win the Ballon d'Or on Monday, earlier produced a sublime assist for 
Alphonso Davies
 to cancel out Eberechi Eze's opener.
But in their biggest test since losing last season's final to Paris Saint-Germain on penalties, Mikel Arteta's 
Premier League
 champions responded well to deservedly return home with a share of the spoils.
A pulsating draw in Bavaria was soured slightly by an early injury suffered by 
Bukayo Saka
.
Noni Madueke
 was recalled in place of Eze following his late winner at 
Nottingham Forest
 on Sunday, with Timber coming in for 
Ben White
 in the Gunners' other change.
Meanwhile, Bayern boss Vincent Kompany was fit enough to take his place on the touchline after a viral infection.
Arsenal
 controlled the early exchanges before suffering a blow when Saka limped off to be replaced by Eze in the 10th minute.
With 
Michael Olise
 suspended, 
Jamal Musiala
 injured and 
Luis Díaz
 only on the bench, Bayern's attacking fear factor was significantly diminished.
Riccardo Calafiori
 threatened from a Martin Ødegaard corner before Arsenal were almost immediately caught out on the counterattack when a goal-kick taken by the quick-thinking Kane culminated in 
David Raya
 saving well from the unmarked 
Serge Gnabry
.
Arsenal continued to dictate proceedings and edged ahead in the 25th minute. Eze was sent racing into Bayern's 18-yard box by Ødegaard's delightful ball over the top before rifling a left-footed shot beyond Neuer and into the bottom-right corner.
Celebrations were briefly halted by an offside flag, only for VAR to overturn the decision, to the delight of almost 3,800 travelling fans high up at the other end of the pitch.
However, Arsenal's lead lasted just 10 minutes.
Kane, who spent much of the game dropping deep, was the architect, producing a sumptuous through ball from inside the centre circle for Davies to outpace Timber and slot past Raya.
For all of their possession, Arsenal appeared vulnerable on transitions and could have gone into the break behind as Kane had an effort blocked before Gnabry picked the wrong option by shooting from distance.
A breathless second half began with a superb save at either end.
First Neuer turned Bruno Guimaraes' header onto the woodwork following a Rice corner, before Raya superbly repelled after 
Dayot Upamecano
 nodded goalwards.
Kane put Bayern ahead with 29 minutes remaining, dispatching his spot kick high into the left of the net at the end of a stuttering runup after seeing a shot blocked by the arm of Timber.
Konrad Laimer
 lashed wide as Bayern came close to doubling their advantage before Rice salvaged a point for Arsenal by latching on to Viktor Gyokeres' pass to slam into the bottom-left corner.
The Gunners almost grabbed a late winner, but Timber's effort was tipped over by Neuer as an entertaining encounter ended all square.
