West Ham
 United have officially announced the launch of a new fan consultation regarding the future direction of the club's official crest. The announcement comes after years of fan dissatisfaction with the current badge, which was introduced in 2016 following the club's move to the London Stadium.

Following the announcement, supporters have taken to social media to share their ideas and desires for the new logo. Several fans have also shared their own concept designs, highlighting a strong preference for a return to a more traditional look.
