Dominik Szoboszlai's Spurs stunner voted LFC Goal of the Month
Dominik Szoboszlai's stunning strike against Tottenham Hotspur has been voted Liverpool's Goal of the Month.
A long-range screamer that caused plenty of debate as to whether it was a volley or half-volley, the effort in the Reds' Carabao Cup win over Spurs at Anfield was the clear No.1 pick in our poll of supporters on Liverpoolfc.com.
Alexis Mac Allister's winner versus Atletico Madrid and Victor Munoz's first goal for the club finished second and third respectively in the vote, which covered August and September.
Another Szoboszlai effort and Cody Gakpo's powerful shot away at Newcastle United on the opening day of the season completed the top five.
