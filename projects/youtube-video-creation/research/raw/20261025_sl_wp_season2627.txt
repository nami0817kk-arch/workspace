The '''2026–27 season''' is the 148th season in the history of [[Sunderland A.F.C.|Sunderland Association Football Club]], and the club's second consecutive season in the [[Premier League]]. In addition to the [[2026–27 Premier League|domestic league]], the club is also participating in the [[2026–27 FA Cup|FA Cup]], the [[2026–27 EFL Cup|EFL Cup]], and the [[2026–27 UEFA Europa League|UEFA Europa League]].

Last season was the first time in the club's history that they qualified for a European competition through a league position, and this is also their first time competing in any European competition since the [[1973–74 European Cup Winners' Cup|1973–74 season]].
