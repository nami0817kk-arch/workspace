Chelsea have €137.5m bid accepted for Morgan Rogers
Rogers is set for a medical on Monday ahead of signing a reported six-year contract at Stamford Bridge
Aston Villa have accepted a €137.5m bid from Chelsea for Morgan Rogers, the Press Association understands. Pic: Bradley Collyer/PA Wire.
Jonathan Veal and Robert O'Connor, Press Association
Aston Villa have accepted a £117million (€137.5m) bid for Morgan Rogers from Chelsea, the Press Association understands.
Rogers, who is currently with the England squad in Miami for their World Cup third/fourth play-off with France, is set for a medical on Monday ahead of signing a reported six-year contract at Stamford Bridge.
The 23-year-old has enjoyed a rapid rise at Villa Park following his move from Middlesbrough in January 2024 and helped fire Villa to a top-four finish and the Europa League last season.
Villa, who also sold Youri Tielemans to Manchester United for £35million earlier in the week, feel the fee represents a good deal.
