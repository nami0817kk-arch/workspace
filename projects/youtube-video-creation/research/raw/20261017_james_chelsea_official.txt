
Injury update: Reece James | News | Official Site | Chelsea Football Club
                    Chelsea Football Club has released the following update regarding Reece James...
Captain 
Reece James
 has undergone successful minor knee surgery.
The 26-year-old was absent from Saturday’s 5-1 victory over Bournemouth at Stamford Bridge while medical assessments took place following the injury suffered in training. Those assessments confirmed surgery would be the required course of action.
Reece has already begun his road to recovery and will be supported by the club's medical department at Cobham during this rehabilitation phase.
Everyone at Chelsea wishes Reece a speedy recovery and will support him on his road to return.
