Kylian Mbappé scores 100th goal for Real Madrid in dramatic Clásico win
Kylian Mbappé scored his 100th goal for Real Madrid to give his side a dramatic 2-1 Clásico win over Barcelona on Sunday. 
The record goal from Mbappé came in the 87th minute of the game to give Madrid a priceless lead after Eduardo Camavinga laid off to him in the box for the Frenchman to tuck home into an empty net.
It came after his first of the game, which was a rather fortuitous strike in the first half. A Gerard Martín attempted clearance was hit straight at Mbappé, who then finished past Joan García to give Madrid the lead. 
Raphina had equalized for Barça in the second half, but Mbappé's late strike secured all three points for Madrid and put them just three points behind their rivals at the top of LaLiga. It also handed Barça their first league defeat of the season. 
Mbappé's match-winning brace comes a day before the 2026 Ballon d'Or ceremony, an award for which the Madrid superstar is very much in the running.
Mbappé has become the 24th player to reach a century of goals for Spain's most successful club and the fourth fastest, joining the likes of Alfredo di Stéfano, Raúl and Cristiano Ronaldo, who sits top of the rankings with 450 goals in 438 matches.

Despite being LaLiga's top scorer in both of his full seasons at the club, Mbappé is yet to win any trophies with Madrid, having lost out on the LaLiga title to Barcelona in both 2024-25 and 2025-26.

The Frenchman joined Madrid on a free transfer in June 2024 after scoring 256 goals in 308 appearances for Paris Saint-Germain and 27 in 60 for AS Monaco.
The 27-year-old also became France's all-time leading scorer, as well as the top scorer in World Cup history, this summer.
