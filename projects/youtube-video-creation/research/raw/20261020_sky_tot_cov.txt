Tottenham 3-1 Coventry: Dejan Kulusevski returns to action as Spurs secure first Premier League win of the season
Report and free match highlights as Tottenham beat Coventry 3-1 at home; Spurs secure first win of the Premier League season thanks to goals from James Maddison, Omar Marmoush and Ben Davies; Jack Rudoni netted for Coventry; Dejan Kulusevski made his return after 526 days sidelined
Monday 19 October 2026 22:56, UK
Please use Chrome browser for a more accessible video player
FREE TO WATCH: Highlights from Spurs' match against Coventry in the Premier League
Tottenham claimed their first Premier League win of the season by beating Coventry 3-1 as Dejan Kulusevski made his long-awaited return after 526 days out injured.
Spurs were winless across their opening six games heading into this fixture but a week in the Marbella sunshine and a well-needed performance on home turf have helped address the doom and gloom, with brighter skies in N17 possibly on the horizon.
James Maddison, making his first competitive start for Spurs since May 2025, announced his return to the starting line-up in emphatic style, curling his opener into the top corner against the club where his professional career began.

The playmaker was influential throughout, seamlessly linking the £300m pieces of Roberto De Zerbi's squad that had previously looked disjointed and awkward, but Jack Rudoni's equaliser just four minutes into the second half punctured the positivity brewing inside the stadium.
No side has dropped more points than Spurs from winning positions at home in the Premier League since the start of the 2024/25 season (30), but Omar Marmoush's first goal for the club, arrowing a fine strike into the far corner, put them on track to ensure they would not suffer the same fate on this occasion.
The feelgood factor that comes with a win skyrocketed in the 85th minute when fan-favourite Kulusevski made his comeback off the bench, bringing an end to his lengthy spell on the sidelines.
Image:
Dejan Kulusevski made a welcome return to action for Spurs
Ben Davies' surprise effort with the outside of the boot rounded off the victory, on what could prove to be the evening that kickstarted the 2026/27 season for Spurs.
They move level on six points with Coventry and above their opponents on goal difference into 16th, having previously come into the fixture rock bottom of the table, and will now be tasked with building on their victory against London rivals Chelsea when the two sides meet at Stamford Bridge, live on
Saturday Night Football.

Is Maddison the missing piece that can link the £300m puzzle?
Sky Sports' Patrick Rowe:
"James Maddison was the star of the show and a big reason Spurs were able to secure the well-needed win. His goal speaks for itself.

"But the rest of his play also stood out, in a slightly more understated manner.
"He was dropping deep, finding pockets of space, threading passes for the runs of Dominic Solanke and threatening from the half spaces with his crossing ability.
"Spurs, after all their investment in the summer, looked disjointed and uninspiring across the opening six games.
"Maddison looks like the piece that could link the £300m pieces of this Spurs puzzle together. Whether that is through his own finishing or the ability to bring other players into the game.
"Keeping him fit and firing is now essential to the club building on this game."

"James Maddison definitely has more panache than Conor Gallagher in that No 10 position.
