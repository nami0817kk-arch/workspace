The '''1999 Intercontinental Cup''' was an [[association football]] match played on 30 November 1999 between [[Manchester United F.C.|Manchester United]], winners of the [[1998–99 UEFA Champions League]], and [[Sociedade Esportiva Palmeiras|Palmeiras]], winners of the [[1999 Copa Libertadores]]. The match was played at the neutral venue of the [[National Stadium (Tokyo, 1958)|National Stadium]] in [[Tokyo]] in front of 53,372 spectators.

Manchester United won the match 1–0, with captain [[Roy Keane]] taking advantage of a mistake by Palmeiras goalkeeper [[Marcos (footballer, born 1973)|Marcos]] to score the winning goal. This was United's only [[Intercontinental Cup (1960–2004)|Intercontinental Cup]] triumph, having been beaten by [[Estudiantes de La Plata|Estudiantes]] in the [[1968 Intercontinental Cup|1968 competition]]. This game was Palmeiras' only appearance in the Intercontinental Cup. [[Ryan Giggs]] was given the [[man of the match]] award.<ref>{{cite web|first=Marcelo |last=Leme de Arruda |title=Toyota Cup - Most Valuable Player of the Match Award |url=https://www.rsssf.org/tablest/toyotamvp.html |publisher=Rec.Sport.Soccer Statistics Foundation |date=2 January 2009 |access-date=12 January 2009 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110629224208/http://www.rsssf.com/tablest/toyotamvp.html |archive-date=29 June 2011 |url-status=live }}</ref> Manchester United's victory made them the first and only team from [[England]] and also the [[British Isles]] to win the Intercontinental Cup.<ref>{{cite news |title=Manchester United 1 Palmeiras 0: Minute by minute |url=https://www.theguardian.com/football/1999/nov/30/newsstory.sport7 |work=guardian.co.uk |publisher=Guardian News and Media |date=30 November 1999 |access-date=7 June 2011 }}</ref>
