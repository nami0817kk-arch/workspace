## 2. 紋章（確度：クラブ公式の発表／背景）

- **1972年の公募**：Wikipedia「In 1972, the crest was redesigned as the result of a competition, won by the Treasurer of the Supporters Club, John Gammage.」
  出典は旧クラブ公式 'The Club Badge'（web.archive.org に保存。**アーカイブは 429 で開けなかった**）。
  本人の文は whatsbehindthebadge.com が「クラブのサイトの文を一字一句写した」と明記して全文を載せている：
  「Therefore one had to concentrate on a familiar object which is exclusive to our county ... I immediately thought of the Suffolk Punch horse.」
  「I regarded the Suffolk Punch as a noble animal well suited to dominate our design and represent the club. To complete the badge, I thought of the town of Ipswich which contains many historical buildings, including the Wolsey Gate and it is close to the sea with a large dock area」
  （同ページ：ギャミッジは 1993/94 季に亡くなった。→ **台本では触れない**）
  HFK も「The badge itself had been introduced in 1972 following a competition and featured a "Suffolk Punch"」。
- **1995年の描き直し**：Wikipedia「modified in 1995 after consultation with a Supporters Forum, with the turrets of the Wolsey Gate moved to the top of the crest, the yellow background changed to red, the Suffolk Punch given a more dominant physique and the F.C. expanded to Football Club」
- **2020-21 に1972年の紋章が戻る（クラブ公式）**：「For the first time since 1995, Town's home kit carries the historical badge which adorned the shirts on that famous night in Amsterdam. The one-off embroidered crest, which includes celebratory 'UEFA CUP 40 YEARS' insignia, is also added to the 2020/21 home goalkeeper kit too.」
- **三つの星**：Wikipedia（出典は旧クラブ公式の記事）「Three stars were added to the sleeve of the team's away shirt for the 2004–05 season, and also to the home kit for the 2005–06 season ... relocated directly above the crest ... 2007–08 ... moved again in 2022–23 to the back of the shirt.」
- **食い違い（採らない）**：Wikipedia は「shirts did not sport a crest until the mid-1960s」、HFK は「crest ... IAFC embroidered diagonally」（1880年代）・「town crest worn between 1933 and 1957」。
  **1972年より前は「市の紋章をもとにした図柄」までしか言わない**（両方が一致する範囲）。
