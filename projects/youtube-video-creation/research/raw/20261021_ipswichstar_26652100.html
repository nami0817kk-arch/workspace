Ipswich Town - Gary O'Neil on what he likes about Jaden Philogene

 
Ipswich Town FC
Football
Sport
Ipswich
By Stuart Watson Chief Football Writer

0 Comments
Jaden Philogene has made one Premier League start and two sub appearances since returning from injury. (Image: Ross Halls)
This article is brought to you by our exclusive subscriber partnership with our sister title USA Today, and has been written by our American colleagues. It does not necessarily reflect the view of The Herald.

Ipswich Town manager Gary O'Neil has explained why he selected Jaden Philogene for his team's most recent home game as the battle for left-wing minutes hots up.

Daizen Maeda, an £8m summer signing from Celtic, started the first three Premier League game of the season out on that side, with Jack Clarke, the Blues' 16-goal top scorer in last season's promotion campaign, subsequently getting the nod for trips to Crystal Palace and Everton.

READ MORE: Assessing Abdoul - How summer signing Ouattara has caught the eye at Portman Road

Philogene, having recovered from a pre-season injury set-back, came into the team for a home win against Fulham, with Clarke playing a big role off the bench in a dramatic late turnaround victory.

O'Neil then switched to a more pragmatic 3-4-2-1 system for last weekend's game at league leaders Manchester City, with Maeda, operating as the right-sided No.10, racing away to score a fine equaliser at the Etihad.

"I really like out-and-out wingers - I like my wide players to play wide, to be honest," explained O'Neil.

"I like that Clarkey brings something different to that and can do that as well.

Abdul Fatawu has started five of the six Premier League games he's been available for. (Image: Ross Halls)

"But the fact that we had no Abdul Fatawu on the right side against Fulham [suspended] meant, for me, that we were desperate for an out-and-out wide winger on the left in that game.

"Jaden had trained well and he's getting back to real sharpness.

READ MORE: European football returns to Portman Road tonight! Here's why - and who Town face

"Daizen's played a more unique, defensive role at times. He's carried that out excellently, but still managed to be a threat.

"I like them all. We've got good options."

Jack Clarke has impacted games both as a starter and sub. (Image: Steve Waller)

Ipswich now head into a run of games that O'Neil is likely to attack - Nottingham Forest (home on Friday night), Hull City (A) and Bournemouth (H).

"If we're just picking a game where I want my team to look like my team, I'll pick two really wide wingers either side," he said.

"But of course the Premier League will throw up some other things at times and you have to respect what you're up against.

"To be honest, I much prefer being the more aggressive team and the team that tries to take it to the opposition. We just try and pick the right ones for each game and what it's going to look like."
